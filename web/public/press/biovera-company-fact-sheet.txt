Bio Vera — Company fact sheet

Bio Vera is a registered agricultural brand—not a thin broker between farm and shelf. Producers onboard under one rulebook covering cultivation, authorised input procurement, packing, and traceability through to the buyer.

Vertical network: contracted input suppliers by locality (including town-level partners where rollout allows); orders placed in-app beside field records. Packed units carry printed Bio Vera badges with QR codes opening each batch’s digital product passport; auditors and consumers read the same dossier. Where configuration enables it, batch evidence can include public on-chain anchoring (typically Polygon in production, per operations documentation).

Founded: 2026. Headquarters: Hamburg, Germany.
