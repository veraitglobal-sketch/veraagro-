This frame looks **beyond individual seasons**: reputation with retailers, resilience of the Balkan→EU corridor we rely on, and how farmers capture fair upside when programmes mature.

---

## 1. Strategic pillars

### Premium positioning

Bio Vera grows when buyers trust **repeatable quality**, not hero lots. Long-term investment in:

- Diary discipline  
- Traceable materials  
- Predictable pallet handover  

### Regional depth

Deepening partner density **without** crowding compliance staff or diluting programme rules.

### Technology leverage

Mobile + web flows that reduce admin load for growers (60+ friendly), not more forms for the sake of forms.
