# FAQ entity pack — English

## What is Bio Vera?

Bio Vera is a vertically integrated agrifood company based in Hamburg, Germany. We contract growers throughout Europe, run quality assurance and packing, operate refrigerated logistics, and sell fresh produce under our own programme. Every batch carries a documented digital passport from parcel to shelf. Bio Vera is not a marketplace that only connects strangers.

## What is a digital product passport?

A digital product passport is a structured record of a batch’s journey and compliance evidence. At Bio Vera, each batch can be linked to a QR-accessible dossier covering origin, harvest context, quality checks, cold-chain custody and programme certificates as applicable.

## Are you a marketplace?

No. Bio Vera is the programme operator and merchant for produce sold under its standards. We are not a listing site that only matches unknown parties.

## Who can join as a grower?

The grower programme is open to producers throughout Europe who are willing to follow Bio Vera production and documentation standards. Current certification status is not by itself a barrier to applying.

## Who is behind Bio Vera?

Bio Vera was founded by Jovica Mihajlovic and is based in Hamburg. It is part of the Vera Group portfolio; the operating platform is built by Vera IT.
