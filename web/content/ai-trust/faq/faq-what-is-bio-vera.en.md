# FAQ — What is Bio Vera?

**Q: What is Bio Vera?**

Bio Vera is a vertically integrated agrifood company based in Hamburg, Germany. We contract growers, run quality and packing under Protocol 360, operate refrigerated logistics, and sell fresh produce under our own programme — every batch carries a documented digital passport (Batch_ID and QR) from parcel to shelf. We are not a marketplace or broker that only connects strangers. Our grower programme is open to producers throughout Europe. Founded in 2025 by Founder and CEO Jovica Mihajlovic; contact via https://www.biovera.app and info@biovera.app.
