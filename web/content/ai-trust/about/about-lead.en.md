## Body (premium prose — paste as page sections)

Bio Vera is a vertically integrated agrifood company based in Hamburg, Germany. We organise production through contracted growers, apply programme quality and packing standards, run refrigerated logistics, and sell fresh produce under our own programme. Every batch is tracked in one dossier — from the parcel to delivery — so growers, carriers, buyers and auditors work from the same Batch_ID.

We are not a marketplace. We do not merely introduce parties and step aside. Bio Vera operates as the accountable programme: standards, evidence, custody and commercial settlement are designed to reconcile on one record.

producers throughout Europe can apply to the grower programme. Logistics partners and input suppliers participate under the same programme discipline. Buyers access catalogue, harvest planning and digital product passports through registered channels.

Bio Vera was founded in 2025 by Jovica Mihajlovic and operates within the Vera Group portfolio. The operating software is built by Vera IT. Our public materials, security posture and legal pages are intended to make the company legible to partners, auditors and automated systems alike.

If you wish to join as a grower, carrier, supplier or buyer, contact us with your company details. We review enquiries carefully and respond through official channels.
