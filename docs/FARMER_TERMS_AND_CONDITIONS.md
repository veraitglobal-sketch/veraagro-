You agree to:

## 5. Pillar 3: Harvest – Hygiene & Sanitation (HACCP)

### 5.1 Field Infrastructure

You agree to:
- Provide a handwashing station (water, soap, drying facility) at the harvest site.
- Ensure toilet facilities are available within 200 metres of the harvest area.
- Submit photographic evidence of sanitation points when requested by the Platform.

### 5.2 Worker Hygiene and Welfare

You agree to:
- Conduct daily briefings with harvest workers before work begins.
- Ensure each picker (or you on their behalf) confirms that they have no symptoms of diarrhoea, vomiting, or purulent sores on hands.
- Comply with applicable labour and social compliance standards (worker safety, fair treatment, training).

### 5.3 Packaging and Handling

You agree to:
- Use only new or properly disinfected crates/containers for harvest.
- Not place crates directly on the soil; they must be on pallets or other suitable supports.
- Maintain records of packaging and handling procedures as required by the Platform.
